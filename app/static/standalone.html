Music for your photo

Drop in a photo. It works out what the photo looks like, decides what that should sound like, and finds songs to match from 11,870 tracks. Everything runs in your browser — your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Songs

    How it works. One model reads images, a different one understands music, and they have never met. A vocabulary of 40 “vibes” joins them: each has a description of how it looks and one of how it sounds. The photo is matched on the first; the music is retrieved on the second.
    Why there is no server. The music model only ever describes the 40 vibes and the 11,870 songs — none of which change — so all of that was computed once, in advance. The only thing your photo needs is the image model, and that runs here.
    Audio. Previews stream directly from Apple. Nothing is stored or served here.
