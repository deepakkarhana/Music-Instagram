Drop in a photo. It works out what the photo looks like, decides what that should sound like, and finds songs to match — from a catalogue of 11,870 tracks.

    How it works. One model reads images, a different one understands music, and they have never met. A vocabulary of 40 "vibes" joins them: each vibe has a description of how it looks and a description of how it sounds. The photo is matched on the first, the music is retrieved on the second.
